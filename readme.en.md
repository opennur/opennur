# OpenNur Project  
### Cahaya Ilmu yang Terbuka | The Open Light of Knowledge

**Bismillahirrahmanirrahim.**

OpenNur is an open-source project for developing Islamic software — essentially a **digital waqf**. Every line of code written here is intended as *sadaqah jariyah* — ongoing charity whose rewards keep flowing even after the author is gone.

---

## Why We Use Technology

Honestly, we've all wondered at some point: *"What's the point of all this advanced technology for religious stuff?"*

But here's the thing — it's a blessing from Allah, and it'd be a shame to only use it for scrolling social media or meaningless chatter. Technology is an incredibly powerful tool, and it's a waste if Muslims don't take advantage of it.

Back in the day, building a prayer time app meant writing code from scratch, dealing with astronomical data, and debugging all night. Now? You just use the development tools available, and within minutes you've got a prototype. Pretty nice, right?

Our goal is to channel this technology toward things that benefit the ummah. Building apps for learning the Quran, understanding Nahwu Shorof, accessing tafsir, memorizing prayers — anything that brings us closer to Allah.

## Vision

To become a collective movement of Muslims leveraging open-source technology to build an Android app ecosystem that is beneficial, transparent, and free from commercial interests.

---

## Core Principles

1. **Open Source**  
   Source code is published on GitHub/GitLab under GPL v3. Anyone can read, learn, modify, and redistribute.

2. **Beneficial**  
   Every feature must serve a real purpose — improving worship quality and religious understanding, not just chasing trends or profits.

3. **Trustworthy**  
   No personal data tracking, no ads, no hidden agendas. User privacy comes first.

4. **Scholarly**  
   Religious content (tafsir, hadith, fiqh) is sourced from recognized authorities. When possible, involve scholars or students of knowledge for review.

5. **Simple**  
   The interface is kept minimal so everyone can use it — including older folks who aren't tech-savvy.

## 🚀 Featured Project

### [Tahsin Qur'an](https://github.com/opennur/tahsin)

An Android app for **Quran recitation practice and review** — featuring:

- 📖 **Digital Uthmani mushaf** (Amiri font), all 114 surahs offline, Arabic + ID/EN translations.
- 🎙️ **Real-time pronunciation assessment** via microphone: words turn green (correct), red (wrong), yellow (currently being read).
- 🎨 **Automatic tajweed coloring**: mad, ghunnah, qalqalah, ikhfa', iqlab, idgham, lam jalalah — toggleable.
- 🔊 **Reciter audio** per ayah & per word (Minshawy, Husary, Alafasy, etc.) at 0.5×–1.25× speed.
- 🔁 **Flow Mode** for hands-free review — automatically advances to the next ayah.
- 🧠 **Tajweed Quiz**: guess the tajweed rule on random words (4 multiple choice + explanation).
- 📖 **Quran Vocabulary**: 589 curated words with SRS system + quiz.
- 🎮 **Dream BIG**: endless vocabulary arcade — high scores & streaks saved.
- 📚 **Learn Arabic**: 15 original lessons (Durusul Lughoh methodology) for beginners.
- 📊 **Integrated stats**: combined across all learning activities.
- 🔍 **Verse search** (Arabic & ID/EN translation) offline across all 114 surahs.
- 🗓️ **Widget & "Ayah of the Day" notifications** — one verse changes daily, offline.
- 🌙 **Dark mode** & **ID/EN language toggle**.
- 🎨 **Custom design system** (no Material 3) — lightweight & distinctive.

> ⚠️ This is a **practice tool**, not a replacement for a teacher. Speech recognition only reads text — it doesn't assess makhraj or harakat length.

## Roadmap

1. **Offline Prayer Time App**  
   - Prayer time calculation based on location (GPS/manual).  
   - Adhan notifications with voice options.  
   - Qibla direction, Hijri calendar, and important dates.  
   - Fully offline, no ads, no unnecessary permissions.

2. **Digital Quran App**  
   - Arabic text with colored tajweed.  
   - Indonesian translation and several other languages.  
   - Memorization mode (muroja'ah) with ayah repetition.  
   - Concise tafsir from trusted sources.

3. **Interactive Nahwu-Shorof Learning App**  
   - Step-by-step modules from beginner to advanced.  
   - I'rab and tasrif exercises with automatic correction.  
   - Modern tool assistance for generating example sentences and extra exercises, validated manually by experts.

4. **Daily Dhikr & Prayer App**  
   - Morning/evening dhikr, daily prayers, and post-prayer supplications.  
   - Reminder features and digital tasbih counter.  
   - Sourced from authentic hadith collections.

5. **Islamic Tech Ecosystem (Exploration)**  
   - Islamic Q&A assistant powered by modern technology, directed to reference the Quran, hadith, and authoritative books.  
   - Automatic fact-checking for hadith claims.  
   - Classical book translation assisted by modern technology.  

   *Important note: technology is not a replacement for scholars. Output must always be verified by competent humans.*
